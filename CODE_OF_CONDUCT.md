# Code of Conduct

VideoHoarder contributors are expected to keep collaboration professional, respectful, and focused on the software.

We welcome constructive bug reports, questions, reviews, and contributions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information/credentials are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, users, or the project. Security-sensitive information should be reported privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.
