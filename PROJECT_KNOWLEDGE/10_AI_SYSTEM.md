# AI System

VideoHoarder supports local Ollama generation and manual ChatGPT exchange. It fingerprints transcript/evidence, creates package manifests, validates hashes and provenance, imports results, records feature coverage/history, and stages reviewed outcomes. The application—not ChatGPT—retains authority over local evidence and physical changes.

Verified by tests: checksum tamper detection, evidence provenance indexing, timestamp parsing, planner integrity/grouping, duplicate review safety, and clip-plan validation/manual handoff.

Needs review: prompt/version migration, cost/token controls at scale, complete result application semantics, stale-evidence invalidation, and real multi-package lifecycle.
